Privacy Policy — SG Rain Now

Last updated: July 19, 2026

SG Rain Now ("the app") is a Singapore rain nowcast utility. This page explains what data the app handles and who it is shared with. The app is provided by an individual developer based in Singapore ("we", "us").

Summary

  • No account is needed and the app never asks you for personal details.
  • Your location is used only on your device to pick the nearest forecast area. The app does not send your location to our servers — we do not operate any servers.
  • The app shows ads via Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. These Google services collect some device data, described below.

Location

The app may request approximate (coarse) location permission. Location is used entirely on your device to select the closest rain region and forecast area. It is not stored by the app beyond that use and is not transmitted to us. You can decline or revoke the permission at any time in system settings; the app still works with a manually chosen area.

Weather data requests

Forecast data is fetched from Singapore's open government data APIs (data.gov.sg). These requests carry standard network metadata (such as your IP address) to that service, but no identity or precise location from the app.

Third-party services

The app includes the following Google services, which collect device data under Google's own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — serves the banner ad. May collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and ad-interaction data for ad delivery and fraud prevention.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics (screens viewed, feature usage, device model, OS version).
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports (stack traces, device state at the time of a crash).

See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob policies for how Google handles this data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any user data ourselves, so there is nothing for us to delete on request. Data collected by the Google services above is retained under Google's retention policies; analytics data is retained for a limited period and crash reports are kept only as long as needed for stability work. Uninstalling the app stops all collection.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect.
