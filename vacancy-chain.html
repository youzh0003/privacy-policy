Privacy Policy — Vacancy Chain: Crab Puzzle

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Which app and developer this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to the following Android application and its developer, as listed on Google Play:

  • App name: Vacancy Chain: Crab Puzzle (listed on Google Play as "Vacancy Chain: Crab Puzzle"; in Simplified Chinese as "换壳中介：寄居蟹解谜")
  • Package name / application ID: com.yzy.vacancychain
  • Google Play listing: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yzy.vacancychain
  • Developer name (Google Play developer account): yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore
  • Contact email: yzyapps99@gmail.com

Vacancy Chain: Crab Puzzle ("the app") is a single-player logic puzzle game that plays offline: you move hermit crabs into bigger shells, in the right order, until every crab fits. This page explains what data the app handles. The app is provided by yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We collect no data. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising SDK. Your gameplay data (progress and moves) stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • One optional one-time purchase, "All Reefs", is processed by Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app receives the purchase details from Google Play to verify and acknowledge the unlock, and stores only that the unlock is owned (see Purchases).
  • The app has no server of its own. It uses the network only to talk to Google Play for the purchase and to check, on your device, whether your Google account owns it.
  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads and no tracking across apps. The app does not use or collect an advertising ID.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission. The app requests only Google Play billing (for the purchase) and internet / network state (so Google Play Billing can reach Google Play).

What is stored on your phone

The app keeps a save file in its own private storage on your device: which puzzles you have solved, the fewest moves you solved each in, and the last puzzle you played. It never leaves your phone, and we never receive it. The app's data is excluded from device backups. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

The app is free to download, and chapter 1 (12 puzzles) is free to play. All Reefs is an optional one-time purchase (no subscription) that unlocks chapters 2 and 3. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing: Google takes the payment, and we never receive or store your card number, billing address, name, email or any other payment detail.

To know whether the unlock is owned, the app asks Google Play on your device whether your Google account owns it (for example when you open the game, return to it, or buy it). Google Play answers with the purchase details (such as the product, the purchase state and a purchase token). The app uses them only to verify the unlock and to acknowledge the purchase back to Google Play, which Google requires to complete it. Of these details the app keeps only a small "owned / not owned" flag in its private storage, so it can open the later chapters offline. The purchase details are not sent to us or to anyone other than Google Play. Google's handling of the purchase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; refunds and your purchase history are managed in your Google Play account.

Who the data is shared with

We do not collect data, so we have nothing to sell or share. Your gameplay data stays on your device. The purchase is processed by Google Play, and the app exchanges the purchase details only with Google Play, as described above.

Children

This app is not directed to children. It is intended for teenagers and adults (13 and over) and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. It does not collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Security

Your save file and the purchase flag stay in the app's private storage, which other apps on the phone cannot read. We hold no server and no database, so there is no account of yours for anyone to break into.

Changes to this policy

If what the app handles ever changes (for example optional usage statistics in a later version), this page changes with it before that version is released, and the date at the top is updated.

Contact

Questions or requests: yzyapps99@gmail.com (yzyy, Singapore).
