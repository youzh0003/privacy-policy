Privacy Policy — Fog Lamp Nonograms

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Which app and developer this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to the following Android application and its developer, as listed on Google Play:

  • App name: Fog Lamp Nonograms (listed on Google Play as "Fog Lamp Nonograms"; in Simplified Chinese as "雾灯数织")
  • Package name / application ID: com.yzy.foglampnonogram
  • Google Play listing: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yzy.foglampnonogram
  • Developer name (Google Play developer account): yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore
  • Contact email: yzyapps99@gmail.com

Fog Lamp Nonograms ("the app") is a single-player picture logic puzzle game (nonograms) that plays offline: you solve puzzles to bring back the pictures of a fog-bound harbour town and its story. This page explains what data the app handles. The app is provided by yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore ("we", "us").

Summary

  • The app collects no data. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising SDK, and it sends nothing about you or your play to us or to anyone else.
  • One optional one-time purchase, "Unlock all plates", is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details (see Purchases).
  • The app has no server of its own. It uses the network only to talk to Google Play for the purchase and to check, on your device, whether your Google account owns it.
  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads and no tracking across apps. The app does not use or collect an advertising ID.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission. The app requests only Google Play billing (for the purchase) and internet / network state (so Google Play Billing can reach Google Play).

What is stored on your phone

The app keeps a save file and your settings (language, cell size) in its own private storage on your device. This includes your game progress: which puzzles you have solved, the marks on puzzles you have started, and the chapter you are on. It never leaves your phone, and we never receive it. The app's data is excluded from device backups. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

The app is free to download, and chapter 1 (9 puzzles) is free to play. Unlock all plates is an optional one-time purchase (no subscription) that unlocks chapters 2–6. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing: Google takes the payment, and we never receive or store your card number, billing address, name, email or any other payment detail.

To know whether the unlock is owned, the app asks Google Play on your device whether your Google account owns it (for example when you open the game, return to it, or buy it). The answer stays on your phone: the app keeps only a small "owned / not owned" flag in its private storage so it can open the later chapters offline. Nothing about the purchase is sent to us. Google's handling of the purchase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; refunds and your purchase history are managed in your Google Play account.

Who the data is shared with

Nobody. We do not collect data, so there is nothing to sell or share. The purchase is handled by Google Play, as described above.

Children

This app is not directed to children. It is intended for teenagers and adults (13 and over) and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. It does not collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Security

Your save file and the purchase flag stay in the app's private storage, which other apps on the phone cannot read. We hold no server and no database, so there is no account of yours for anyone to break into.

Changes to this policy

If what the app handles ever changes (for example optional usage statistics in a later version), this page changes with it before that version is released, and the date at the top is updated.

Contact

Questions or requests: yzyapps99@gmail.com (yzyy, Singapore).
