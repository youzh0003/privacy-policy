Privacy Policy — Mossjar

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Which app and developer this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to the following Android application and its developer, as listed on Google Play:

  • App name: Mossjar (listed on Google Play as "Mossjar"; in Chinese as "苔瓶小客")
  • Package name / application ID: com.yzy.mossjar
  • Google Play listing: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yzy.mossjar
  • Developer name (Google Play developer account): yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore
  • Contact email: yzyapps99@gmail.com

Mossjar ("the app") is a single-player cozy game that plays offline: you decorate a small jar of moss, tiny visitors come to stay, and you turn the jar to find them and take their photo for your album. This page explains what data the app handles. The app is provided by yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We receive no data about you or your play. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising SDK, and we run no server that it sends to. The only data that leaves your phone is what the Google Play Billing component exchanges with Google: purchase and ownership information, and Billing technical diagnostics. The ownership check runs when you open or return to the game, even if you never buy (see Purchases).
  • One optional one-time purchase, which unlocks two more jars (Dry Garden and Pond Corner), is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details (see Purchases).
  • The app has no server of its own. It uses the network only through Google Play Billing: to make the purchase and to check, on your device, whether your Google account owns it.
  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads and no tracking across apps. The app does not use or collect an advertising ID.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission. The app requests only vibrate (small haptic taps during play), Google Play billing (for the purchase) and internet / network state (so Google Play Billing can reach Google Play).

What is stored on your phone

The app keeps a save file in its own private storage on your device: your jars and where you placed each decoration, the visitors you have met, your album, your dewdrops, the sound setting, and your device's time-zone offset (read from the phone's clock so the jar shows day, golden hour or night at the right local time). The in-game photos in your album (the album cover and the visitor stickers) are pictures of the game's own jar, saved as image files in the same private storage; the app does not use the phone's camera and does not save anything to your gallery. None of this leaves your phone, and we never receive it. The app turns off Android's cloud backup for its data (Android may still copy it if you move to a new phone with a direct device-to-device transfer). Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

The app is free to download, and the Moss Forest jar is free to play. The two extra jars are an optional one-time purchase (no subscription). The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing: Google takes the payment, and we never receive or store your card number, billing address, name, email or any other payment detail.

To know whether the extra jars are owned, the app asks Google Play on your device whether your Google account owns them (for example when you open the game, return to it, or buy). Google Play answers with your purchase record, including a purchase token; after a purchase the app sends that token back to Google Play to acknowledge it, as Google Play requires. The app does not store the token. It keeps only a small "owned / not owned" flag, and the time Google Play last answered, in its private storage so it can open the extra jars offline. Nothing about the purchase is sent to us. The Google Play Billing library built into the app may also send Google technical information about its own connection to Google Play (for example errors); we do not receive it. Google's handling of the purchase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; refunds and your purchase history are managed in your Google Play account.

Who the data is shared with

We receive no data, so we have nothing to sell or share. The only data exchanged with anyone is between the app's Google Play Billing component and Google: purchase and ownership information (including the purchase token) and Billing technical diagnostics, as described above. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

This app is not directed to children. It is intended for teenagers and adults (13 and over) and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not receive personal information from anyone, children included; purchases are handled by Google Play as described above.

Security

Your save file, album pictures and the purchase flag stay in the app's private storage, which other apps on the phone cannot read. We hold no server and no database, so there is no account of yours for anyone to break into.

Changes to this policy

If what the app handles ever changes (for example optional usage statistics in a later version), this page changes with it before that version is released, and the date at the top is updated.

Contact

Questions or requests: yzyapps99@gmail.com (yzyy, Singapore).
