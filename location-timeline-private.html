Privacy Policy — My Location Timeline

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Which app and developer this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to the following Android application and its developer, as listed on Google Play:

  • App name: My Location Timeline
  • Package name / application ID: com.yzy.locationtimelineprivate
  • Google Play listing: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yzy.locationtimelineprivate
  • Developer name (Google Play developer account): yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore
  • Contact email: yzyapps99@gmail.com

My Location Timeline ("the app") keeps a private, automatic diary of where you go: it records your location in the background and shows you, for any day, the routes you travelled and the places you stopped. This page explains what data the app handles and who it is shared with. The app is provided by yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore ("we", "us").

Summary

  • Your location history is stored only on your phone. It is never uploaded, never sent to us, and never shared with anyone. We do not operate any servers and could not read your timeline even if we wanted to.
  • The app records location in the background, including when the app is closed — that is its purpose, and it tells you so in a notice before it ever asks for location permission. Recording only runs while you have switched it on, and a persistent notification is shown whenever it is running.
  • No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting software.
  • The only network-capable component in the app is Google Play's billing service, used solely for the one-time Pro purchase. Your location data never travels over the network.

Location data

While recording is switched on, the app collects your device's precise location and, through Android's activity-recognition service, the kind of movement detected (such as walking or driving — used to tell journeys apart from stops). From this it builds journeys, visited places, and daily routes.

All of it is written to a database in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never transmitted anywhere — not to us, not to any third party. The app contains no code that sends location data over a network, and its recording engine runs entirely on the device.

Recording is under your control: it starts only after you tap "Start recording" and accept the background-recording notice, and you can stop it at any time from the app's home screen. While it runs, Android shows the app's ongoing-recording notification and the system location indicator.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Precise location (and background location) — to record your routes. Background ("Allow all the time") access is what lets the timeline keep recording when the app is closed; without it, recording only works while the app has recently been open, and the app says so honestly rather than pretending otherwise.
  • Physical activity — to detect automatically when a journey starts and ends.
  • Notifications — the persistent "recording is on" notification, and nothing else. Notifications never contain your location.
  • The app also uses standard system capabilities to keep recording reliable: a foreground service marked as location use, permission to resume after your phone restarts, and a wake lock so recordings are saved correctly.

Storage, retention and deletion

Everything lives in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Free version: a rolling 90 days of history is kept on the device and older records are deleted automatically; the calendar lets you view the most recent 7 days.
  • Timeline Pro (one-time purchase): your full history is kept for as long as you like, and you can export it.
  • If Pro stops being active on your Google account (for example after a refund), history older than the free window is not deleted immediately: the app warns you first and gives you a grace period of at least 30 days, during which you can export that history for free before it is removed.
  • Your timeline is excluded from Android device backups, so it is not copied into your Google account backup. Moving to a new phone means using the app's export.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes your entire timeline. There is no copy anywhere else.

Export

With Pro, you can export your timeline as a GPX or CSV file. You choose where the file is saved using Android's system file picker; the app writes the file there and keeps no other copy. An exported file contains your location history in readable form — the app reminds you of this before every export, and how you store or share that file is up to you.

Network use

The app makes no network requests of its own. Its manifest carries the network permission only because the Google Play billing component described below requires it. Your location data is never sent over any network connection.

Purchases

The one-time Timeline Pro unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and we never receive or store your payment details. Whether you own the unlock is stored on your device and re-checked with Google Play, so it survives reinstalling the app.

No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting

The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK, and it does not collect an advertising ID. We receive no usage data of any kind from the app. Your device's own system-level reporting to Google (such as Android's crash and diagnostics settings) is separate from the app and governed by your device settings and Google's privacy policy.

Third-party services

The app includes exactly one third-party service:

  • Google Play Billing — handles the one-time Pro purchase. Data involved in the purchase is processed by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy.

There are no other SDKs, no analytics services, and no data-sharing partners.

Data retention and deletion requests

We do not hold any of your data ourselves, so there is nothing for us to delete on request — your timeline exists only on your phone, where you can delete it by uninstalling the app. Purchase records are held by Google Play under Google's retention policies.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect.
