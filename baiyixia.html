Privacy Policy — 拜一下：擲筊・燒金紙

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Which app and developer this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to the following Android application and its developer, as listed on Google Play:

  • App name: 拜一下：擲筊・燒金紙 (listed on Google Play in Traditional Chinese as "拜一下：擲筊・燒金紙" and in English as "拜一下: Moon Blocks & Joss Paper"; romanised Bai Yi Xia). These are the same app, published from one Google Play listing under the package name below. This policy covers it under either name.
  • Package name / application ID: com.yzy.baiyixia
  • Google Play listing: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yzy.baiyixia
  • Developer name (Google Play developer account): yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore
  • Contact email: yzyapps99@gmail.com

拜一下 ("the app") is a digital folk-culture experience: you can throw a pair of moon blocks (擲筊) and burn paper offerings (燒金紙) on your phone. Throw results are a random simulation made by the app. The app is provided by yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore ("we", "us"). This page explains every way data can leave your device.

Summary

  • What you write stays on your phone. The question you type before a throw and your question journal (問事簿) are stored on your device and excluded from Android backup and device transfer. We never upload them. If you choose to include your question on a keepsake card and share it, that text goes to the app or person you choose (see Keepsake cards).
  • The app sends a fixed list of usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase and crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics (see Analytics and Crash reports). These never contain your question, which recipient you burn paper for, your journal marks or anything you type.
  • Three optional "Support the developer" one-time purchases are processed by Google Play (see Purchases).
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no account, no sign-in. We have no server of our own.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or notification permission. The app asks for no runtime permission at all.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your questions and journal: when you write a question and throw, the app records the question, the time, the mode and the results in a journal on your device. You can mark entries and delete them. The app never uploads this file, and it is excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Your settings (sound, vibration, shake to throw, reduce motion, language and appearance choices) are stored on your device. If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may include these settings in your Google account backup; Google stores and encrypts that backup and we cannot read it.
  • Your purchase record: the unlock status, the IDs of supporter products you own, and purchase tokens still waiting to be acknowledged, kept in a separate file that is not backed up.

Keepsake cards

After three 聖杯 in a row or a standing block, the app can make a keepsake picture on your device. Your question is left off the card unless you switch it on. When you tap share, the picture goes through Android's share sheet to the app or person you pick; we do not receive it.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase, Google)

The app defines only these custom events and adds no custom parameters and no free text to them (Firebase also collects the automatic data described below):

  • throw_single, throw_triple_round (a throw was made, in one-throw or three-in-a-row mode)
  • result_sheng, result_xiao, result_yin, result_li (which of the four results came up)
  • triple_done (three 聖杯 in a row)
  • burn_session_1_2, burn_session_3_7, burn_session_8_14, burn_session_15_plus (how many sheets were burned in a session, as a range; never for whom)
  • share_card (the share button was tapped; it does not confirm that anything was shared), support_open (the support page was opened)

The Firebase SDK also collects, by itself: an Analytics app-instance ID; a Firebase installation ID (FID), which identifies this installation to Firebase services; device and session data (device model, OS version, app version, language, first-open and engagement time); an approximate location (country / region / city level) that Google derives from the IP address; how the app was installed (Google Play install referrer); and its own standard events such as first open, session start, screen views, app updates and in-app purchases (which product was bought and its price, used as purchase history). No advertising ID is collected; advertising and ad-personalisation features are switched off. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Google processes it for us as a service provider under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Google Analytics keeps event-level data for the retention period set for our Analytics property (2 months by default); expired data is removed by Google's regular deletion process, and aggregated reports may be kept longer. We do not sell this data or share it with anyone else.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, Google)

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: stack trace, device model, OS version, app version and the time of the crash, with a random Crashlytics installation UUID. Crash reports contain no question text and no journal content. Crashlytics begins removing crash data and associated identifiers from its live and backup systems after 90 days.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app is free. "Support the developer" offers three optional one-time purchases (no subscription); any one of them unlocks all appearance options, and they differ only in the amount. Google Play takes the payment; the app never receives your card number or billing address. To know whether a purchase is owned, the app asks Google Play on your device; it uses the answer only to unlock the appearance options and to acknowledge the purchase back to Google Play, and keeps the purchase record described under What is stored on your phone. Purchases belong to your Google account and can be restored after reinstalling. Refunds and your purchase history are managed in your Google Play account under the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete journal entries in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device. The app has no user accounts and never asks for your name or email, but Analytics and crash data are linked to the installation identifiers described above. Uninstalling does not immediately delete data already sent to Google; it expires on the schedule above. For help, write to yzyapps99@gmail.com.

Children

This app is not directed to children. It is intended for adults and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects ever changes, this page changes with it before that version is released, and the date at the top is updated.

Contact

Questions or requests: yzyapps99@gmail.com (yzyy, Singapore).
