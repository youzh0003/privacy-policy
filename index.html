Privacy Policy — Kiddie Flashcards (renaming to Kids Flashcards: 9 Languages)

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Which app and developer this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to the following Android application and its developer, as listed on Google Play:

  • App name: currently listed on Google Play as "Kiddie Flashcards". A rename to "Kids Flashcards: 9 Languages" has been submitted and is awaiting Google Play review; this policy covers the app under either name. The listing and package are unchanged.
  • Package name / application ID: com.yzy.kidsflashcards
  • Google Play listing: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yzy.kidsflashcards
  • Developer name (Google Play developer account): yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore
  • Contact email: yzyapps99@gmail.com

Kiddie Flashcards ("the app"), also listed as Kids Flashcards: 9 Languages once the pending rename is approved, teaches young children animals, plants, the alphabet, numbers, colours, shapes and the world around them, in up to nine spoken languages, using picture flashcards, sounds, quizzes and short illustrated stories. This page explains what data the app handles and who it is shared with. The app is provided by yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore ("we", "us").

Summary

  • The app is made for children. Its declared target age groups on Google Play are 5 and under and 6–8, so it is subject to Google Play's Families policy and we design it accordingly.
  • No account is needed. The app never asks a child or a parent for a name, email address, phone number, photo or any other personal detail. The only way we ever learn who you are is if a parent chooses to email us feedback (see below).
  • The app requests no sensitive Android permissions. Its only permission is internet access, used to download flashcard pictures and audio and to show ads.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are configured as child-directed and non-personalised, and are limited to general-audience (G-rated) content.
  • The app sends anonymous usage counts and crash reports through Google Firebase so that it can be fixed and improved.
  • We do not sell data, and we do not operate any servers of our own.

Children's privacy and the Families policy

Because the app is directed to children under 13, we take these positions and have configured the app to enforce them in code, not merely to promise them:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no fields in which a child could enter personal information, no profile, no chat, no sharing feature and no in-app purchases.
  • Ads are not personalised. The app tells the Google Mobile Ads SDK to apply child-directed treatment (TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE) and caps ad content at the G rating, so ads are contextual rather than based on a profile of the user, and no advertising profile is built from the child's use of the app.
  • The analytics SDK is prevented from collecting the advertising identifier and from sending ad-personalisation signals (google_analytics_adid_collection_enabled=false and google_analytics_default_allow_ad_personalization_signals=false).
  • We aim to comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and, for children in the EU/EEA and UK, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe the app has collected information about your child that you would like removed, email yzyapps99@gmail.com and we will act on it — see Your choices and how to reach us below.

What data the app handles

The app itself asks for nothing about you. The data below is collected automatically by the Google services listed in the next section, and matches the Data safety declaration on the app's Google Play listing:

  • Crash logs — if the app crashes, a report containing the device model, Android version and a diagnostic stack trace is sent so the defect can be found and fixed.
  • Diagnostics — basic performance and error information of the same kind.
  • App interactions — anonymous, aggregated counts of which screens, card categories and features are used, so that the app can be improved. These are tied to a random installation identifier, not to a person.
  • Device or other identifiers — identifiers used by the Google services below to deliver ads, cap how often an ad is shown and detect fraud or invalid traffic. As described above, they are not used to personalise ads in this app.
  • IP address — like any internet request, downloading pictures, audio and ads carries the device's IP address to the Google service being contacted. An IP address can indicate approximate location such as a country or city; the app never requests, reads or transmits the device's GPS location, and holds no location permission at all.

None of this includes a child's name, voice, photographs or any content created in the app, because the app collects none of those things.

Third-party services used by the app

The app includes the following Google services, which collect data independently of us and under their own terms. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

  • Google AdMob — serves the ads in the app. May receive device information, identifiers and IP address for ad delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention. Configured for child-directed treatment and G-rated, non-personalised ads, as described above.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — records the anonymous usage events described above, with advertising-identifier collection and ad-personalisation signals switched off.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — records crash and error reports.
  • Firebase Storage — hosts the flashcard pictures and audio that the app downloads while you use it. These are ordinary file downloads; the requests carry the device's IP address to Google, and no information about the child is uploaded.
  • Google Play In-App Review — used only when the app offers the standard Play review prompt. Any review you choose to write is handled by Google Play, not by the app.

We can see the reports these services produce in Google's own dashboards, in aggregate form. We do not receive, store or have any way to look up an individual child's data, and we do not combine this information with anything else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell or rent data to anyone.
  • It does not show personalised or interest-based advertising.
  • It does not ask for or hold location, microphone, camera, contacts, storage or photo permissions.
  • It has no login, no user profile, no chat and no user-generated content. The "share" button only passes a short message and a link to the app to Android's own share sheet, where you choose what to do with it; nothing about your use of the app is included and nothing is sent to us.
  • It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

If you email us feedback

The app has a feedback option intended for parents. It does not send anything by itself: it opens your own email app with a message already addressed to yzyapps99@gmail.com, and nothing reaches us unless you decide to send it. If you do send it, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use that only to reply to you and to fix what you reported, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we delete the correspondence once it is no longer needed. Please don't include personal details about your child in it — we don't need any to help you.

Data retention

The crash reports and anonymous usage events are held by Google under Firebase's own retention settings and are deleted according to them. We keep no separate copy of any of it, and we have no database of our own. Uninstalling the app stops all further collection.

Your choices and how to reach us

  • You can reset or delete the device's advertising identifier at any time in the Android settings, under Privacy → Ads, and on many devices you can switch off ad personalisation system-wide.
  • Uninstalling the app ends any further data collection by it.
  • Parents and guardians may email us to ask what has been collected about their child, or to ask us to delete it. Because the data described above is not tied to a name or an account, please tell us the approximate date and the device model so we can ask Google to locate and delete the matching records. We will reply within 30 days.
