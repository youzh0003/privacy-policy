Privacy Policy — SayBoard

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Which app and developer this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to the following Android app and its developer:

  • App name: SayBoard: AAC Text to Speech (Chinese titles: 替我说 SayBoard：文字转语音 AAC / 替我說 SayBoard：文字轉語音 AAC), referred to below as "SayBoard" or "the app"
  • Package name / application ID: com.yzy.sayboard
  • Google Play listing: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yzy.sayboard
  • Developer name (Google Play developer account): yzyy
  • Contact email: yzyapps99@gmail.com

"We" and "us" below mean the developer, yzyy.

Summary

SayBoard has no accounts, no ads and no usage statistics, and we have no server. The app does not send what you type, your phrases, your history or your settings to us. The only information we may receive is described under "Purchases" (order records provided by Google Play) and "Emails you send us". Some features work through other apps or services on your phone (the speech engine, Android backup, a place you choose for an exported file, and Google Play for the optional purchase); these are described below.

What SayBoard does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, usage statistics or crash-reporting tools.
  • No developer server: nothing you type or save is sent to us by the app.
  • The app does not request the Android internet permission or the network-state permission. The optional purchase goes through Google Play billing.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, microphone, camera or photos.

Speech

Sentences are read out by the text-to-speech engine on your phone, which is a separate app (for example, Speech Services by Google). SayBoard passes it the text to read. SayBoard only uses voices that the engine reports as installed on the phone and not needing a network connection. How the engine app handles data is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

History

SayBoard keeps up to the latest 200 things you have had it speak, on this phone only. Speaking the same text again straight away updates its time instead of adding a new entry. You can pause recording or clear History at any time on the History page. History is never included in Android backups or in exported backup files. You can export History as a text file yourself; see "Exported files".

Phrases and settings

Your phrases, categories and settings are stored on your phone.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include your phrases, categories and settings in your device backup (for example, Google's backup service) or in a transfer to a new phone. Whether this happens depends on those settings, and the backup is handled by Android and the backup provider under their own terms and privacy policies, not by us. History and purchase status are never included.

Exported files

You can export your phrases (a backup file) or your History (a text file). An exported file is plain text and not encrypted. You choose where it is saved; if you choose a cloud drive or another app, the file goes there under that service's terms. Anyone who has the file can read it. Importing reads only the file you pick.

Purchases

SayBoard's speaking features are free. The one optional purchase (supporter colours) is handled by the Google Play Store app, which connects to Google to complete and confirm it. The app does not receive your card or other payment details. Google Play tells the app the status of the purchase (for example, whether it has been bought), and the app keeps a small record of that status on your phone; this record is not included in Android backups. Google's handling of the purchase is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. Google Play provides developers with order records for purchases in the Google Play Console. We use them only to handle refunds and support requests.

Crash and performance information

SayBoard contains no crash-reporting tool. Separately, if you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, your Android device may report how apps run on it, such as crashes, freezes and performance, to Google. Google shows developers this information in the Google Play Console as "Android vitals", for example crash and "app not responding" reports grouped by issue, with breakdowns such as device model and Android version. This information is collected by Google under Google's own policies, not by SayBoard.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply. We do not add you to any list. Ask us and we will delete the conversation.

Children

SayBoard is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Your choices

Clearing SayBoard's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes the phrases, settings, History and purchase status that the app keeps in its own storage. Files you exported and any Android backup are kept separately: delete them where you saved them, or in your phone's or Google account's backup settings. To have emails you sent us deleted, ask us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and update the privacy page inside the app in the next version.

本隐私政策适用于以下 Android 应用及其开发者：

下文的「我们」指开发者 yzyy。

概要

替我说没有账号、没有广告，也不收集使用统计；我们没有服务器。本 App 不会把你打的字、你的短语、历史或设置发给我们。我们可能收到的信息只有「购买」一节说的订单记录（由 Google Play 提供）和「你发给我们的邮件」。有些功能经由手机上的其他 App 或服务完成（语音引擎、Android 系统备份、你为导出文件选择的位置、可选购买所用的 Google Play），见下文。

替我说不做的事

  • 没有账号，不需要登录。
  • 没有广告，不使用广告 ID。
  • 没有统计、使用数据收集或崩溃上报工具。
  • 没有开发者服务器：你打的字、存的内容都不会被本 App 发给我们。
  • App 不申请 Android 联网权限和网络状态权限。可选购买通过 Google Play 结算完成。
  • 不读取位置、通讯录、麦克风、相机或相册。

朗读

句子由手机上的语音引擎读出来，它是另一个 App（例如 Google 的语音服务）。替我说只把要读的文字交给它，并且只使用引擎报告为「已安装在手机上、不需要联网」的声音。引擎 App 怎样处理数据，以它自己的隐私政策为准。

历史

替我说在这台手机上最多保留最近 200 条朗读记录；紧接着再读同一句话，只更新时间，不新增一条。你可以随时在历史页暂停记录或清空。历史不会进入 Android 系统备份，也不会进入导出的备份文件。你可以自己把历史导出为文本文件，见「导出的文件」。

短语和设置

你的短语、分类和设置保存在你的手机上。

Android 系统备份

如果手机设置里开启了备份，Android 可能会把你的短语、分类和设置放进设备备份（例如 Google 的备份服务），或在换新手机时一起迁移。是否进行取决于你的系统设置；备份由 Android 和备份服务提供方按它们自己的条款和隐私政策处理，不经过我们。历史和购买状态永远不包括在内。

导出的文件

你可以导出短语（备份文件）或历史（文本文件）。导出的文件是明文，没有加密。存到哪里由你选择；选了网盘或其他 App，文件就按那个服务的条款存到那里。拿到文件的人都能看到里面的内容。导入时只读取你选中的那个文件。

购买

替我说的说话功能都是免费的。App 里唯一的可选购买（支持者配色）由 Google Play 商店处理，由它连接 Google 完成和确认。本 App 不会收到你的银行卡或其他付款信息。Google Play 会告诉本 App 购买状态（例如是否已购买），本 App 在手机上保存一条很小的状态记录，它不进入系统备份。Google 对购买的处理适用 Google Play 服务条款和 Google 隐私政策。Google Play 会在 Play 管理中心向开发者提供购买的订单记录，我们只用它处理退款和用户求助。

崩溃和性能信息

替我说不含任何崩溃上报工具。另外，如果你选择了与 Google 共享使用情况和诊断数据，你的 Android 设备可能会把应用的运行情况（如崩溃、无响应和性能）报告给 Google。Google 在 Play 管理中心以「Android vitals」的形式向开发者展示这些信息，例如按问题归类的崩溃和「应用无响应」报告，并可按设备型号、Android 版本等细分。这些信息由 Google 按它自己的政策收集，不是本 App 收集的。

你发给我们的邮件

如果你给我们发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容，只用于回复你，不会把你加入任何名单。你要求的话，我们会删除这些往来邮件。

儿童

替我说面向成年人（18 岁及以上），不以儿童为对象。

你的选择

在手机设置里清除替我说的存储空间，或卸载 App，会删除本 App 在自身存储里保存的短语、设置、历史和购买状态。你导出的文件和 Android 系统备份是分开保存的：请到你存放的位置删除，或在手机、Google 账号的备份设置里处理。如需删除你发给我们的邮件，请告诉我们。

本政策的变更

如有变更，我们会更新本页面和「更新日期」，并在下一个版本中同步更新 App 内的隐私页。
