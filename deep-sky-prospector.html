Privacy Policy — Deep Sky Prospector

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Which app and developer this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to the following Android application and its developer, as listed on Google Play:

  • App name: Deep Sky Prospector (referred to below as "the app" or "the game")
  • Package name / application ID: com.yzy.deepskyprospector
  • Google Play listing: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yzy.deepskyprospector
  • Developer name (Google Play developer account): yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore
  • Contact email: yzyapps99@gmail.com

Deep Sky Prospector is an idle space-mining collection game: drones mine an asteroid belt while you are away, and a daily spectrometer reveal identifies the crystals they bring back for a species codex. This page explains what data the app handles and who it is shared with. The app is provided by yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore ("we", "us").

Summary

  • No account and no sign-in. The app never asks you for a name, an email address, a phone number, a photo or any other personal detail.
  • Your game save never leaves your device. There is no cloud save, no leaderboard, no multiplayer and no server of ours for it to go to — we operate no servers at all.
  • The app shows no ads. There is no advertising SDK in the build, and no advertising identifier is collected — the advertising-ID permissions that Google's analytics library would otherwise add have been removed from the app's manifest and advertising-ID collection is switched off in code.
  • There are no purchases. The app contains no billing code: nothing in it can be bought, and no payment sheet exists.
  • The app requests no runtime permissions — no location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts, no storage, no photos, no notifications.
  • The app does send anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage counts through Google Firebase so that defects can be found and the game improved. That is the whole of the data collection.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone, and there is no advertising or marketing use of any of it.

What data the app handles

The app itself asks for nothing about you. The data below is collected automatically by the two Google services listed in the next section, and it matches the Data safety declaration on the app's Google Play listing:

  • Crash logs — if the app crashes or hits an error, a report containing the device model, the Android version and a diagnostic stack trace is sent so that the defect can be found and fixed.
  • Diagnostics — basic performance, session and error information of the same kind (for example how long a session lasted, or that the app started).
  • App interactions — coarse, aggregated counts of which screens and features are used (for example that a scan was performed or the codex was opened), so that the game can be improved. These are tied to a random installation identifier, not to a person.
  • Device or other identifiers — a random, app-specific installation identifier (the Firebase installation ID and the analytics app instance ID) that lets the services below tell one installation from another, so that a crash report and the events around it belong together. It is not the device's advertising identifier — the app does not collect that one — and it is discarded when the app is uninstalled.
  • IP address — like any internet request, the crash and analytics uploads described above carry the device's IP address to the Google service being contacted. An IP address can indicate approximate location such as a country or city; the app never requests, reads or transmits the device's GPS location, and holds no location permission at all.

All of it is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Your game save

Your progress — ore, crystals, the species you have identified, the pieces on your display case, your ship, decor and settings — is stored only in the app's own private storage on your device, as a single local file. It is never uploaded, never backed up to us and never shared with any third party. If Android's own device backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include that file in your personal Google backup under Google's terms, not ours. Uninstalling the app deletes the save.

Third-party services used by the app

The app includes exactly two Google services, which collect data independently of us and under their own terms. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

  • Firebase Crashlytics — records the crash and error reports described above (stack traces and device state at the time of the crash).
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — records the anonymous usage events described above, with advertising-identifier collection switched off (google_analytics_adid_collection_enabled=false) and the advertising-ID permissions removed from the app's manifest.

There is no advertising SDK, no billing library, no social or login SDK, no attribution or marketing SDK, and no analytics service other than the one above. We can see the reports these two services produce in Google's own dashboards, in aggregate form. We do not receive, store or have any way to look up an individual player's data, and we do not combine this information with anything else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • It does not show ads of any kind, personalised or otherwise, and it collects no advertising identifier.
  • It contains no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no billing code.
  • It does not ask for or hold location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or photo permissions.
  • It has no login, no user profile, no chat, no friends list, no leaderboard and no user-generated content. Nothing you do in the game is visible to any other player.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Deep Sky Prospector is a general-audience game intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, its Google Play target-audience declaration excludes the under-13 age groups, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and that information about them was collected, email yzyapps99@gmail.com and we will act on it.

Data retention and deletion

The crash reports and anonymous usage events are held by Google under Firebase's own retention settings and are deleted according to them; we keep no separate copy and we have no database of our own. Uninstalling the app deletes your local game save and stops all further collection.

You may ask us what has been collected about your installation, or ask us to delete it, by emailing yzyapps99@gmail.com. Because the data described above is not tied to a name or an account, please tell us the approximate date and the device model so that we can ask Google to locate and delete the matching records. We will reply within 30 days.
