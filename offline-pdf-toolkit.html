Privacy Policy — Offline PDF Toolkit

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Offline PDF Toolkit ("the app") merges, splits, reorders and password-protects PDF files, and turns images into PDFs. This page explains what data the app handles and who it is shared with. The app is provided by an individual developer based in Singapore ("we", "us").

Summary

  • Your PDFs and images never leave your device. Every tool runs on your phone — nothing is uploaded, and we do not operate any servers.
  • No account is needed and the app never asks you for personal details.
  • The app has no permission to browse your storage. It can only ever see the specific files you pick in the system file picker, or that you send to it from another app.
  • There are no ads, and the app does not collect an advertising ID.
  • The app does send crash reports and basic usage counts so it can be fixed and improved. These are tied to a random installation identifier, so they are not anonymous, and they never include your files, their names or their contents. You can switch this off in the app's About screen.

Your documents

Every operation happens on your device. When you pick a file, the app copies it into its own private storage to work on, writes the result where you tell it to in the system file picker, and deletes the working copy immediately afterwards. Anything left behind by an interrupted operation is deleted the next time the app starts.

The file you pick is never modified — every tool writes a new document and leaves the original exactly as it was.

Your documents, their names and their contents are never sent to us, never included in crash reports or usage counts, and never shared with any third party.

Passwords

A password you type — to open a protected PDF, or to protect a new copy — is held in memory only for as long as it is needed for that operation. It is never written to storage, never included in crash reports or usage counts, never written to system logs, and never sent anywhere.

The app can only remove a password that you supply yourself. It does not attempt to guess, recover or bypass a password it was not given.

Crash reports and usage counts

Unless you switch it off, the app sends:

  • Crash reports — the technical details of a failure, such as the stack trace and the state of the device at the time.
  • Usage counts — which tool was opened, whether it finished, how many files it was given, and if it failed, which of the app's own error categories applied. These are counts and fixed labels; the app provides no way for a file name or path to be recorded.

This information is tied to a random installation identifier generated by Google's software. We do not ask for or store anything that identifies you personally, but because that identifier is consistent for your installation, this reporting is pseudonymous rather than anonymous. We describe it plainly rather than calling it anonymous.

You can turn it off at any time: open the app, go to About, and switch off "Send crash reports and usage counts". Turning it off stops collection immediately, for both crash reports and usage counts.

Network use

The app needs network access for three things only: sending the crash reports and usage counts described above, and confirming a purchase with Google Play. Your documents are never sent over the network. The app works fully offline — every tool continues to work with no connection at all.

Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and we never receive or store your payment details. Whether you own the unlock is stored on your device and re-checked with Google Play, so it can be restored if you reinstall the app.

Advertising

The app contains no advertising, and no advertising SDK. The Android advertising-ID permissions are explicitly removed from the app, so it cannot collect an advertising ID even indirectly through the analytics software it includes.

Third-party services

The app includes the following Google services, which handle data under Google's own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports (stack traces, device state at the time of a crash).
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — the usage counts described above, plus standard device information such as device model and operating system version.
  • Google Play Billing — the one-time Pro purchase.

See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data ourselves, so there is nothing for us to delete on request. Data collected by the Google services above is retained under Google's retention policies: usage data is kept for a limited period, and crash reports only as long as they are useful for fixing problems. Switching off reporting in the About screen stops further collection, and uninstalling the app stops all collection.

Your documents are yours and stay on your device, so deleting them is a matter of deleting the files themselves — the app keeps no copy.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect.
