Privacy Policy — Dusk Fisher

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Which app and developer this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to the following Android application and its developer, as listed on Google Play:

  • App name: Dusk Fisher (listed on Google Play as "Dusk Fisher" in every language)
  • Package name / application ID: com.yzy.duskfisher
  • Google Play listing: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yzy.duskfisher
  • Developer name (Google Play developer account): yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore
  • Contact email: yzyapps99@gmail.com

Dusk Fisher ("the app") is an offline, single-player fishing game: you sail a small boat out at dusk, fish through the night and come home to sell your catch. This page explains what data the app handles. The app is provided by yzyy, an individual developer based in Singapore ("we", "us").

Summary

  • The app collects no personal data and shares no data with anyone.
  • The app does not use the internet. It does not request the Internet permission, has no server, and sends nothing anywhere.
  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking and no third-party SDK that collects data. The app does not use an advertising ID.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission. The only permission the app requests is vibrate, used for short haptic pulses (which you can switch off in Settings).

What is stored on your phone

The app keeps a save file and your settings (language, volume, vibration, reduced motion, frame-rate cap) in its own private storage on your device. This includes your game progress: your boat, upgrades, money, the fish in your hold, your journal and the notes you have found. It never leaves your phone, and we never receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

The app is free to download. A future version may offer an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full game. If it does, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment and we never receive or store your card number, billing address, name or any other payment detail. The app would learn from Google Play only whether your Google account owns the unlock. Google's handling of that purchase is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. This page will be updated before any such purchase is added.

Who the data is shared with

No one. The app does not transmit any data, so there is nothing to share, and we do not sell data.

Children

This app is not directed to children. It is intended for teenagers and adults (13 and over) and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your save file stays in the app's private storage, which other apps on the phone cannot read. We hold no server and no database, so there is no account of yours for anyone to break into.

Changes to this policy

If what the app handles ever changes, this page changes with it before that version is released, and the date at the top is updated.

Contact

Questions or requests: yzyapps99@gmail.com (yzyy, Singapore).
